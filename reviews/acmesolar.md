## Thesis
Every quality gate passes: zero pledge, unmodified audit opinions, cash-generative (CFO 2.5× PAT), receivables controlled at 60 days, FY26 PAT doubled on real commissioned capacity — not accounting. The BESS pivot is genuinely working: 4.96 GWh operational by Oct-2026, merchant/peak revenue at ~₹2.2 cr/day, FY27 BESS guidance revised UP to 10+ GWh. But at ₹433 the market already prices 52× FY26 earnings and ~6× book on a 3.75×-levered balance sheet with 1.6× interest cover — and the engine offers no price discipline because the anchor can't see the business. This is a delivery-and-deleveraging watch, not an entry: the growth is real, the price already knows, and the leverage is the load-bearing risk.
