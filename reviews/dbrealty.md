## Thesis
Every engine gate passes — no distress, clean usability, no event vetoes — but the engine's own valuation model prices this business at ₹22.69 against a ₹90.05 quote: a 297% premium for 0.66% ROE, 2.2% ROCE, and earnings that exist only when a project completes or a one-off lands. FY26's "turnaround" (₹27cr PAT) rode Ten BKC completion and a BMC credit note; the core operation remains a lumpy, low-return land bank funded by serial equity dilution (promoter 63%→47% since FY21) and rising promoter pledges (29%→44.7% in 2026). This is a call option on Mumbai redevelopment execution, not a compounding business — and the option is priced as if already exercised.

## Risks
- **Valuation is the veto-by-arithmetic:** ₹90.05 vs engine FV ₹22.69; a ~78% fall just to reach the first trigger rung
- **Pledge 44.72% of promoter holding** (Jun-2026), up from ~29% in Dec-2025 — one step below the 50% D1 veto; SAST filings show continued equity-backed borrowing (Goenka Family Trust 1.66cr-share pledge, Sep-2026)
- **Marginal profitability:** ROE 0.66%, ROCE 2.19%, IC 1.54× (one weak year from tripping E3); FY24's ₹1,317cr PAT was one-off-driven (other income ₹1,453cr)
- **Extreme revenue lumpiness:** Q4 FY26 sales −84% QoQ to ₹86.9cr with a ₹59cr net loss; project-completion accounting makes any single year unreadable
- **Demerger restatement:** hospitality demerged eff Apr-2025; gross block ₹2,431cr→₹91cr; pre-FY26 history not comparable
- **Governance watch:** independent director Rajeev RA resigned Sep-25-2026 ("no other material reasons" per filing); promoter-family board
- **Serial dilution:** promoter holding 63% (FY21)→47.17% via QIPs/preferentials; dilution risk recurs with every funding round
