## Thesis
Microcap NBFC (₹282cr mcap) priced like a distressed asset but built like a fortress: CRAR 91.0%, D/E 0.13×, zero promoter pledge, profitable every year except the demerger year, FY26 P/E 6.6× and P/B 0.49×. The single load-bearing problem is asset quality — headline GNPA 17.28% at Mar-2026, dominated by one legacy ₹43.97cr Proseed exposure that was fully provided years ago and technically written off in Q1FY27 (legal recovery retained as upside). Adjusted GNPA was 7.66% at FY26-end and the published Q1FY27 print (14-Aug-2026) shows reported GNPA down to 8.9% (₹35.21cr), 99.9% secured with 2.5× coverage. The engine's hard floor is backward-looking by construction; the forward data says the specific cause of the veto is clearing. Not buyable under the veto — but the cleanup is monitorable with dated catalysts, hence WATCH, not PASS.
