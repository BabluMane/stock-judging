## Cash Conversion: Explainable, Not Fraudulent

The 46.6% 5yr CFO/EBITDA triggered the forensic flag, but the breakdown shows:

- **Sales 6.2x** (FY21→FY26: Rs 328cr → Rs 2,047cr)

- **Borrowings barely rose** (Rs 41cr → Rs 117cr) — funded from internal accruals
- **CFO/PAT 81%** — above the 80% bar

This is a legitimate growth capex cycle, not cash leakage. The lumpy pattern matches working-capital absorption during hypergrowth.
