## Thesis
A real specialty-chemicals franchise mid-turnaround — FY26 revenue +32% to ₹506cr, EBITDA margin back to 18.4%, Q1 FY27 revenue +43% YoY, and CRISIL just upgraded the bank facilities to A-/Stable. But it is priced for a transformation the numbers have not delivered: 90× TTM earnings and 5.9× book against 5.4% ROE, free cash flow negative five years running, and asset productivity (revenue per KL of reactor capacity) down ~57% since FY22. The engine values the equity at ₹176 (0.53× book) on 3.4% sustainable growth; the market at ₹1,983 is paying for a permanent return to 20%+ ROE. Quality of the recovery is not in doubt — the price is. This becomes interesting only on a deep derating toward the ₹157–168 trigger zone.
