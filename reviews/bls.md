## Thesis

Global #2 visa-outsourcing franchise with a 40%-margin core at a 13x-TTM price near its 52-week low. Record FY26 (Rs 2,998 cr revenue, Rs 724 cr PAT, 29% ROE, zero pledge, Z'' 7.9, CFO 1.25x PAT) reads like a quality compounder — but the engine vetoes on E4: the effective tax rate has sat below 15% for two straight audited years (FY25 10.9%, FY26 9.2%). That low ETR is structural (SEZ/overseas units), not an accounting red flag, so the veto is mechanical rather than diagnostic — yet the bar is the bar, and even setting the veto aside the price (Rs 222) sits above both entry triggers (ACC Rs 200 / INV Rs 153), so the engine offers no sanctioned entry here. The DCF fair value (Rs 242) itself rests on the spec's 15% OPM fallback against a reported 28% — the valuation is conservative by construction, which cuts both ways.
