## Thesis
India's largest mid-segment hosiery maker (~15% of the organised market) that cannot convert profit into cash. FY26: PAT Rs 106cr but CFO **−Rs 149cr** — the second straight year of negative operating cash flow — while debtor days stretched to 140 and short-term borrowings doubled to Rs 578cr to fund the working-capital hole. The engine trips three independent quality vetoes (E2, E6, D4) on filed consolidated data and excludes it. A live three-way demerger (Vertical A = 47% of revenue into Lux and Cozi Ltd, Vertical C = 11% into Lux Global Ltd, 1:1 entitlement, both to list) adds a comparability break on top of the cash problem. Passing is the right call until cash collection is proven.

- Debtor days at 140; trade receivables Rs 1,126cr = 38% of sales — growth is being funded through the channel
- Short-term borrowings doubled to Rs 578cr in FY26; interest cover down to 4.6×

- Piotroski F=2/9: ROA, leverage, liquidity and turnover all deteriorated FY25→FY26; only profitability and no-dilution score
