## Thesis
Clean regional bank at a distressed-bank price. All gates pass; GNPA has fallen six straight years to 2.50%, CRAR at a record 16.55%, record FY26 PAT ₹2,363cr with RoE 16.85%. Yet it trades at 0.91× book / 6.6× earnings — the market prices J&K concentration risk, 59.4% government ownership, and the Q1 FY27 margin miss. The engine's own ladder puts INV at ₹263; at ₹139 you're already deep in invest territory. The question isn't quality — it's whether RoE survives the NIM trough.
