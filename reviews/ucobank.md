## Thesis
A repaired PSU bank trading below book for a real reason: the government must sell. All gates pass — CAMEL 7/10 with CRAR 18.61% and GNPA 2.17% (down from 9%+ at the Sep-2021 PCA exit), zero pledge, clean usability, no event vetoes. FY26 was the best year on record: PAT ₹2,768 cr (+13.2%), cost-to-income improved 581 bps to 52.66%, and the bank beat its own FY26 guidance on nearly every line. At ₹23.45 you're 7% under the engine's invest trigger at 0.88× book. But this is a lower-quality version of the J&K Bank setup: RoE 8.3% barely covers the cost of equity, the CAMEL efficiency legs score 1, and the 90.95%-to-75% dilution (up to 270 cr new shares + a possible OFS, SEBI deadline already being extended) will cap the price until the supply is absorbed. The anchor says buy the discount; the overhang says don't pay up for it.
