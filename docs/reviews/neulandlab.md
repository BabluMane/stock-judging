## Thesis

Neuland is the best-run company in the promise-tracking universe — credibility **9.6/10**, the highest of 49 audits, with management delivering on nearly every commitment across 8 scored calls. It is a genuine CRAMS compounder (ROCE 21-27%, 17% 5-yr sales CAGR), and Mukul Agrawal's **largest holding by value** (3.1%, Rs 496cr, held steady). The $30M peptide module (operational Sep-2026, second module underway) is a real GLP-1/CDMO option.

But Rs 21,055 — 54x TTM earnings, 14.5x book, ~45x EV/EBITDA — prices a flawless GLP-1 transformation, not the business as it stands. Fundamental fair value is **~Rs 4,300**. The market price implies FY31 revenue of ~Rs 17,800cr (**54% CAGR**). Wonderful company, uninvestable price.
