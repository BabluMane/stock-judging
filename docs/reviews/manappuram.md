## Thesis

India's #2 gold-loan NBFC (Rs 57,006cr gold AUM, +98% YoY) emerging from its worst 2 years. The Asirvad MFI blowup (RBI embargo Oct-2024) crushed FY25-26 earnings (PAT Rs 2,197cr → Rs 993cr, ROE 21% → 7%), but Q1FY27 shows the turn: PAT Rs 585cr (+341% YoY), Asirvad profitable again, gold yields +60bps to 17.7%, Bain Capital's Rs 4,385cr joint-control investment de-risking the balance sheet.

At Rs 312 (1.8x book, 10.3x FY28E EPS) it is fairly priced for a recovery — not cheap enough to buy the uncertainty, too good a franchise to ignore.

- Consolidated GNPA **1.5%** (FY26) — remarkably clean, because gold loans (82% of book) barely default
- Pain was concentrated in Asirvad MFI (GNPA 4.8% at worst, AUM -49% YoY); now profitable again (Rs 21cr Q1FY27)
- Fitch: stage-3 ratio improved to **1.9%** by Q1FY27
