## Thesis

Xpro India (polymer processing, dielectric/Biax films) is mid-way through a massive capex cycle: fixed assets Rs 179cr→Rs 390cr, CWIP Rs 299cr, borrowings Rs 39cr→Rs 325cr. The new Biax dielectric film capacity could be transformative — but the stock at Rs 1,096 (78.7x TTM P/E, 3.38x book, **116x normalized earnings**) prices in perfection. To justify the price at an 11% discount rate, the company needs 2.9x current sales at 12% margins, or margins above the all-time 15% peak. ROCE has collapsed 25%→4%, OPM 15%→6%, other income is 60% of PBT, and FCF is deeply negative two years running. Kacholia's steady 3.9% and C5=2 credibility are genuine positives — but they don't fix a 4–5x overvaluation. Great story, wrong price.

## The Capex Bet — What the Market Is Pricing

- Productive asset base (fixed + CWIP) is **4.6x** what it was in FY24 (Rs 151cr → Rs 689cr) — almost certainly the Biax dielectric film line
- If the new line ramps to 15% ROCE it generates ~Rs 100cr EBIT; at 25x that's ~Rs 900/share — still below current price
- The market needs **more** than full utilization: record margins on record volumes, simultaneously
- Borrowings up 8x in 3 years; interest coverage is thin if the ramp is slow

## Earnings Quality Flags

- **Other income 60% of PBT** (Rs 18cr vs Rs 30cr FY26) — core operating earnings far weaker than headline; persistent (38% FY25, 17% FY24)
- **Possible interest capitalization** (screener flag) — legitimate during construction (CWIP Rs 299cr) but flatters P&L until it ends
- **Promoter holding declined** 45.26%→40.32% over 3 years, alongside equity dilution (12→23cr capital) to fund capex
- **Cash conversion cycle jumped** to 80 days (FY26) from 42 — working capital deteriorating into the capex cycle
