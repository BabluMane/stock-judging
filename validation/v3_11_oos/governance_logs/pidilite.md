# Pidilite Industries — governance-event log (v3.11 OOS)
Scrip: BSE 500331 / NSE PIDILITIND. Coverage: events 2017-04-01 → 2023-03-31 (later items informational only). Scoring dates 2019-03-31, 2021-03-31. Method: web searches (SEBI order/settlement/show-cause, Parekh family/promoter conduct, auditor resignation/qualification, gift/off-market/inter-se transfers; only the first four queries ran because the session's WebSearch budget was then exhausted); Pidilite's own website (annual reports FY2017-18 to FY2022-23, MGT-9/MGT-7, and its stock-exchange-announcements archive of Reg 7(2)(b) Form C letters to BSE/NSE, FY2017-18 to FY2019-20) fetched directly; annual reports keyword-scanned for qualified/adverse opinion, auditor resignation, SEBI penalty/stricture, show-cause; scanned Form C letters read as images where the text layer was unreadable. BSE/NSE announcement pages and API returned 403/Access Denied, so exchange filings were seen only as the company-website copies of what it sent to BSE/NSE. Key limitation: the annual-report Change-in-Promoters'-Shareholding tables (MGT-9) exist only for FY2017-18 to FY2019-20; from FY2020-21 the company publishes MGT-7 with no transaction list, its archive holds no promoter Form C letters after FY2019-20, and I could not reach BSE/NSE Reg 7/29/10(6) filings or run further searches. THEREFORE PROMOTER-GROUP GIFT/OFF-MARKET TRANSFERS FROM 2020-03 ONWARD (including the whole 12 months before the 2021-03-31 scoring date, Apr-2020 to Mar-2021, and FY2021-22/FY2022-23) ARE NOT SOURCED, not shown to be absent. Pidilite's promoter family (Parekh) makes frequent intra-family gifts; under the README's literal rule ("off-market transfer/gift of promoter shares, including inter-se transfers structured as gifts") each disclosed gift is a promoter_conduct row, although each is an intra-promoter-group family gift (rows say where promoter-group holding did not change, or did fall). No SEBI/ED/SFIO action, auditor resignation or qualified opinion, pledge invocation, arrest/charge-sheet, withdrawn capital action or rating action to D was found for Pidilite (absence of search hits, not proof of absence; annual reports FY2017-18 to FY2022-23 state no SEBI/stock-exchange penalties or strictures and show no qualified opinion).

Dating convention for gifts: event date = the date of the Pidilite letter to BSE/NSE enclosing the Form C where I read it; where I did not reach the letter, the transaction date from the annual-report table is used and the row says so (actual public disclosure could be a few days to ~6 weeks later; the one lag I could verify was Harshada Vakil, 2019-02-22 gift disclosed 2019-04-08).
