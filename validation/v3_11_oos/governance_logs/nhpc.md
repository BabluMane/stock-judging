# NHPC Ltd — governance-event log (v3.11 OOS)
Scrip: NSE NHPC / BSE 533098. Coverage: events 2017-04-01 → 2023-03-31 (scoring dates 2019-03-31, 2021-03-31). Promoter is the President of India (Government of India, ~70%+ holding), so promoter pledge-invocation and off-market/gift transfer categories are not applicable in the ordinary sense; the Government's share sales (Bharat 22 ETF transfers, Jun 2018 / Feb 2019) are on-market/ETF transfers and are non-qualifying. Method: web-search queries (SEBI order / show-cause / penalty; CBI / ED / arrest / charge-sheet of officials; statutory-auditor qualification and CAG comments) plus direct reading of the company's own Annual Reports for FY2017-18 to FY2022-23 (BSE-hosted PDFs: standalone and consolidated statutory-auditor reports, CAG s.143(6)(b) comments, corporate-governance report, secretarial-audit reports, credit-rating section). Not reachable: BSE/NSE corporate-announcement pages (not browsable through the proxy), so no exchange filing was read directly; the Annual Reports are company filings hosted by BSE. The web-search budget for the session was exhausted (200 calls) part-way through the NHPC pass, so no separate searches were run for NHPC on: ED/SFIO, statutory-auditor resignation, ratings actions on subsidiaries (the Annual Reports show AAA/stable domestic ratings from India Ratings, ICRA and others FY2019-20 to FY2022-23), and Subansiri / Teesta / arbitration developments (context only, not logged as events). Absence of hits is not proof of absence. CAG-appointed statutory auditors: distinction applied here between (a) statutory-auditor opinion (FY2017-18 to FY2022-23 all UNMODIFIED on standalone and consolidated statements, some with emphasis-of-matter paragraphs) and (b) CAG s.143(6)(b) comments (NIL / "nothing significant" every year read, FY2017-18 to FY2022-23). CBI / CVC / vigilance actions are outside the §2.1 literal list and logged as non-qualifying. No SEBI / ED / SFIO action against NHPC, its promoter or KMP was found; exchange (BSE/NSE) LODR fines are exchange actions, not SEBI orders, and are logged as non-qualifying.
