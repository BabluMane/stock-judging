# Srei Infrastructure Finance Ltd (incl. Srei Equipment Finance Ltd) — governance-event log (v3.11 OOS)
Scrip: BSE 523756 / NSE SREINFRA (SIFL). Consolidated group notably includes Srei Equipment Finance Ltd (SEFL, 100% subsidiary, unlisted equity, listed/rated debt). Scoring date 2020-03-31; 24m window 2020-04-01 to 2022-03-31; coverage 2017-04-01 to 2026-09-29 (events dated after 2022-03-31 are marked POST-WINDOW and are informational only). Method: WebSearch/WebFetch plus direct download and text extraction of primary PDFs: Acuité press releases (2021-01-15, 2021-03-05), CARE press releases (2020-12-14; the 2024-03-01 SIFL and SEFL releases whose Annexure-2 rating-history tables give every CARE action date from 2020 to 2023), Brickwork press releases (2021-04-06, SEFL and SIFL), the SEBI adjudication order on SIFL (PDF, 2022-03-29), the NCLT Kolkata order in RBI v. SIFL (IBBI copy, pronounced 2021-10-08), SIFL NSE filings (Q3 FY21 outcome 2021-02-13, Q1 FY22 outcome 2021-08-14) and the SIFL FY2021-22 annual report (BSE). National press (Business Standard amp pages fetched directly, Moneylife, BusinessToday, Goodreturns) was used for dates not in primary documents. NOT reachable: BSE/NSE announcement pages (not browsable; only a handful of NSE archive PDFs surfaced via search results), srei.com (503 or connection reset), rbi.org.in press-release page (RBI supersession date/grounds taken from Srei's Reg 51 intimation as reported plus Mondaq and Business Standard), careratings.com 2021 originals (the CARE 2021-03-06 date is taken from CARE's own 2024 rating-history annexure, the SEBI order which cites "CARE press release dated March 06, 2021", and Srei's 2021-03-06 exchange filing as reported). The WebSearch budget for the session was exhausted mid-task, so the 2017-2019 pass (SEBI/ED/SFIO, pledge invocation, off-market transfers, auditor resignation) is partly a headline-scan of Business Standard topic pages (Srei, SIFL, SEFL, Kanoria; roughly 2017 to 2023) and not exhaustive; absence of hits is not proof of absence. Promoter holding shown flat at 60.80% for Mar-2019 through Mar-2022 (Screener), with no pledge data shown; pledge creation/invocation could NOT be verified either way. No Deloitte forensic audit was found; the forensic audits found were KPMG (appointed by SEFL/lenders 2021-04-24; final report Jan 2022) and BDO India as transaction auditor for the Administrator (2022). Rating agencies checked: CARE, Acuité, Brickwork (D actions found); ICRA (only withdrawal documents found, no D), India Ratings and CRISIL (nothing found for 2020-21). Rating actions above 'D' (Sep-2019 AA to A+, Nov-2020 to Dec-2020 BB/BB+ cuts) are non-qualifying and listed below. Key finding on the seed: the NCLT order of 2020-12-30 (SEFL scheme, interim moratorium 2021-01-01 to 2021-06-30) barred rating agencies from treating non-payment as default; NCLAT stayed that clause on 2021-03-02, after which the D actions followed (Acuité 2021-03-05, CARE 2021-03-06, Brickwork 2021-04-06).
