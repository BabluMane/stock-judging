# Gayatri Projects Ltd — governance-event log (v3.11 OOS)
Scrip: NSE GAYAPROJ / BSE 532767. Scoring dates 2019-03-31 and 2020-03-31 (24m windows to 2021-03-31 and 2022-03-31). Coverage: events 2017-04-01 → 2026-09-29; events dated after 2022-03-31 are informational only (marked INFORMATIONAL in the description). Method: the CARE Ratings press-release archive for the company was enumerated through CARE's own site search and every press release was downloaded and read (2017-2026); Business Standard (fetched directly) for press dating; Gayatri's own investor-relations page (Reg 30 PDFs, incl. scanned letters read as images) and its Annual Report FY2019-20 and FY2022-23; the Trendlyne mirror of the company's SAST Reg 31 / insider-trading pledge filings for pledge-invocation dates. NOT reachable: BSE/NSE announcement pages (not browsable), SEBI order database (site search returned errors/530), ICRA/India Ratings/CRISIL/Brickwork/Acuité per-issuer histories (no working issuer search; a CRISIL rating-rationale listing search for "Gayatri" showed no Gayatri Projects entry). The WebSearch tool hit its session cap (200/200) part-way through, so the SEBI/ED/auditor/off-market-transfer checklist items rest on the reachable primary documents above and were NOT confirmed by an independent news sweep; absence of hits is not proof of absence. Consolidation check (AR FY2019-20): subsidiaries are Gayatri Energy Ventures Pvt Ltd (wholly owned) and Bhandara Thermal Power Corporation Ltd (step-down); Gayatri Highways Ltd (formerly Gayatri Domicile) is an ASSOCIATE (not consolidated as a subsidiary), and Gayatri Hi-Tech Hotels / Gayatri Sugars / Gayatri Developwell are promoter-group entities (not subsidiaries). No D rating on GEVPL, BTPCL or GHL was found. Blind to prices/outcomes; nothing here judges outcomes. Events 2015 and earlier (e.g. CARE D of 2015-11) are outside coverage.
