# Sadbhav Engineering Ltd — governance-event log (v3.11 OOS)
Scrip: NSE SADBHAV / BSE 532710. Scoring dates 2020-03-31 and 2021-03-31 (24m windows to 2022-03-31 and 2023-03-31). Coverage: events 2017-04-01 → 2026-09-29; events dated after 2023-03-31 are informational only (marked INFORMATIONAL). Method: (1) CARE Ratings' full press-release archive was enumerated through CARE's own issuer search for Sadbhav Engineering (SEL), Sadbhav Infrastructure Project Ltd (SIPL) and 16 SPVs (Rohtak-Hissar, Rohtak-Panipat, Aurangabad-Jalna, Bhilwara-Rajsamand, Dhule-Palesner, Hyderabad-Yadgiri, Nagpur-Seoni, Ahmedabad Ring Road, Ahmedabad-Maliya [group membership not verified; not in SEL's FY19 AOC-1 list], Sadbhav Bangalore, Bhimasar Bhuj, Kim, Nainital, Udaipur, Vidarbha, Vizag) and every 2017-2023 release was downloaded and its rating action read (D flagged automatically, then read manually); (2) SEL's own investor page (https://www.sadbhaveng.com/investors/): ~480 Reg 30 announcements (titles scanned 2017-2026; the rating, auditor, non-compliance, restructuring and litigation letters opened), annual reports FY2017-18 to FY2022-23 (text extracted: subsidiary lists, audit reports, contingent-liability notes), audited-results filings for FY22 and FY23; (3) Business Standard (fetched directly) for press dating of India Ratings and CRISIL actions; (4) CRISIL's public rating-rationale listing. CONSOLIDATION: SIPL is a consolidated subsidiary of SEL throughout (SEL holds ~69.7% per Business Standard/Ind-Ra July 2021; SEL AR FY19: "27 subsidiary companies (including step-down subsidiaries)", FY20 and FY21: 19), and every SPV below is a step-down subsidiary listed in SEL's AOC-1 (FY19) — RHTPL and RPTPL are called "step-down subsidiaries of the Company" in SEL's own FY22 and FY23 audit reports. A D on any of them therefore counts under type 7. Several operating SPVs were sold by SIPL to IndInfravit Trust in 2019-20 (SEL Reg 30 letters 2019-10-23, 2019-10-25, 2020-02-15, 2020-03-16), but none of the sold SPVs shows a D in CARE's archive. NOT reachable: BSE/NSE announcement pages (not browsable); SEBI order database (errors/530); ICRA, India Ratings (single-page app, no issuer search reachable), Brickwork and Acuité per-issuer histories; CRISIL's listing exposes only the latest action per issuer (Bijapur-Hungund and Shreenathji-Udaipur upgraded to AA in Aug-2020 then withdrawn May-2022; Bhilwara-Rajsamand withdrawn Jun-2020; Sadbhav Rudrapur "B, issuer not cooperating" 2026-02-24 — no CRISIL D found, but earlier CRISIL history and the Bhavnagar/Una/Gadag/Mysore-Bellary/Jodhpur/Tumkur SPV ratings were not enumerable). The WebSearch tool hit its session cap (200/200) mid-run, so news sweeps for SEBI, ED, income-tax, pledge invocation and auditor resignation could not be completed: those checklist items rest on the reachable primary documents only, and absence of hits is not proof of absence — in particular an India Ratings, ICRA, Brickwork or Acuité D on any group SPV before the CARE dates below cannot be excluded. Blind to prices/outcomes.
