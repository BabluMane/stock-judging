Method: web-search queries (SEBI orders / show-cause / adjudication / settlement / insider trading; whistleblower and forensic-audit coverage; ED / SFIO / MCA; auditor resignation / qualification; promoter pledge invocation and inter-se / off-market transfers; withdrawn buyback / dividend / fundraise; promoter / KMP arrest or charge-sheet; group-company actions, incl. Taro; rating actions) plus fetches of cited pages. Sources actually reached: two SEBI order pages on sebi.gov.in (2017-08-10 settlement order; 2021-02-11 settlement orders, page metadata only, PDF body not readable), Business Standard / Business Today / Zee / Moneylife / Yahoo-Reuters / ThePrint / Medical Dialogues pages (some via fetch, some via search-result summaries). Not reachable: BSE/NSE corporate-announcement pages (not browsable through the proxy), so no exchange filing was read directly; Business Standard pages returned HTTP 403 to fetch and are cited from search-result summaries (the article headline/date is visible in the URL and result). The full SEBI 2021 settlement order text (SCN date, application dates) was not readable; the SCN date of 2020-05-19 is taken from a Medical Dialogues summary of the SEBI order and is consistent with Business Standard / Moneylife wording ("show cause notices in May 2020"). The web-search budget for the session was exhausted during this task (200 searches), so the pass is targeted, not exhaustive; absence of hits is not proof of absence. Statutory-auditor opinions for FY2017-18 to FY2021-22 were NOT read year by year (only searched for resignation / qualification headlines, none found); no audit-report qualification was located. Search on promoter pledge found creation/release only, no invocation. US FDA, DOJ/antitrust (Taro), MCA/RoC and Income-tax matters are outside the §2.1 literal list and are logged as non-qualifying.
