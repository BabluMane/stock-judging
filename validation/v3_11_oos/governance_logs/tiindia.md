# Tube Investments of India — governance-event log (v3.11 OOS)
Scrip: BSE 540762 / NSE TIINDIA. Coverage: events 2017-04-01 → 2023-03-31 (scoring dates 2020-03-31 and 2021-03-31; anything later is informational only and marked as such). Method: web searches on SEBI orders/show-cause/settlement (TI, Murugappa promoters and group companies Coromandel, Parry, Chola, Carborundum, Shanthi Gears, Ambadi Investments), ED/SFIO/CBI, auditor events, promoter pledge/inter-se/off-market/gift, withdrawn capital actions, arrest/charge-sheet, rating actions to D; direct read (BSE-hosted PDFs) of TI's Annual Reports FY2018-19, FY2019-20, FY2020-21 and FY2021-22 for the auditors' reports (standalone and consolidated), the CG Power business-combination note, and penalty/strictures statements; direct read of the SEBI CG Power Final Order (2022-10-04, 248 pp.) and Adjudication Order (2022-12-23, 17 pp.) and of the SEBI Sabero Organics final order (2021-04-19, 54 pp.). IMPORTANT PREMISE CORRECTION: the Murugappa group / Tube Investments was NOT a promoter or associate of CG Power in 2019. TI signed its subscription agreement with CG Power on 2020-08-07 (board approval reported 2020-08-07) and CG Power became a TI subsidiary on 2020-11-26 (TI annual report FY21, Note 40.2). The Sept-2019 SEBI interim order, the Dec-2019 SFIO probe and the Mar-2020 SEBI confirmatory order all pre-date any TI link and are logged as non-qualifying (link did not exist at the event date). From 2020-11-26 CG Power is a consolidated subsidiary documented in TI's own filings, so actions on CG Power after that date can meet the link test of type 6. BSE/NSE corporate-announcement pages and the BSE announcement API were NOT reachable (403/Access Denied); tiindia.com annual-report links hit a CAPTCHA (BSE-hosted copies were used); the SEBI order-search endpoint is behind a WAF. The session's web-search budget ran out before every planned query was run (absence of search hits, not proof of absence — in particular Murugappa group-company checks for Chola/Parry/Carborundum/Shanthi Gears were shallow).
