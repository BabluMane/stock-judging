# Emami Ltd — governance-event log (v3.11 OOS)
Scrip: BSE 531162 / NSE EMAMILTD. Coverage: events 2017-04-01 → 2022-03-31 (later items informational only). Scoring dates 2019-03-31, 2020-03-31. Method: NO web search was run for Emami (the session's WebSearch budget was exhausted during the KEI/Pidilite passes), so this log rests on direct fetches of Emami's own website and annual reports: (a) Emami's statutory-filings page, which hosts the promoter-entity Reg 31 SAST encumbrance disclosures from 2019-11-16 to 2020-03-13; I opened and read (as images) about 12 of the ~24 filings (2019-11-29, 2019-12-13, 2019-12-18, 2020-01-27, 2020-02-11/12/13/17/18/20, 2020-03-03, 2020-03-07, 2020-03-13) - all were Reg 31(1) encumbrance CREATIONS/additional pledges, none an invocation; the rest were not opened (image-only scans); (b) Emami's "in the news" page and two press clippings it hosts (Economic Times 2019-03-01, Business Standard 2020-02-08); (c) buy-back page (2020, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2026 buybacks); (d) annual reports FY2018-19 to FY2021-22 keyword-scanned (FY2017-18 file is AES-encrypted and could not be read, so FY2017-18 auditor report and promoter table are NOT checked); (e) MGT-9 FY2018-19 and FY2019-20 promoter-holding tables. BSE/NSE announcement pages were not fetched (403 pattern seen for these sites elsewhere in this task). No exchange filings before 2019-11-16 on promoter pledges were reachable, so pledge INVOCATIONS in 2018 to early Nov 2019 are not ruled out by source; the ET clipping (2019-03-01) describes pledge REDUCTION after a stake sale, not invocation. No SEBI/ED/SFIO action, auditor resignation, qualified opinion (in window), arrest/charge-sheet, withdrawn buyback/dividend/fundraise or rating action to D was found in what was reached (absence of evidence given the limited search, not proof of absence). Promoter pledges were very large (encumbered shares reached 81.05% of promoter holding in the 2020-03-13 disclosure) but all reached items are creation/additional pledge.
