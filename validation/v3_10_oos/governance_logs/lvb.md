# Lakshmi Vilas Bank — governance-event log (v3.10 OOS)
Scrip: BSE 534690 / NSE LAKSHVILAS (amalgamated into DBS Bank India Ltd, effective 2020-11-27; shares delisted). Coverage: events 2018-03-01 → 2026-09-29. Method: WebSearch plus page fetches of Business Standard (amp pages via curl), The Week, Moneylife, Forbes India, Deccan Chronicle, RBI press release of 2020-11-25 and DBS newsroom. The RBI 2020-11-17 press release/draft scheme PDF was blocked by a CAPTCHA, so moratorium and write-off facts are taken from press coverage of it. The RBI PCA letter itself was not retrieved; LVB's stock-exchange filing is quoted in the press. Post-2020-11-27 there is no listed entity, so nothing after that date except the SEBI review noted below.
